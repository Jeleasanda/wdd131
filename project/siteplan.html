Site Name

Project Humanity

I chose this name because the goal of the website is to explore how technology—specifically AI—can be used to serve humanity, protect the Earth, and promote ethical innovation rather than just corporate greed.

Site Purpose

The site will serve as an educational hub and a call to action. It will provide resources on ethical technology, explain the dangers of unchecked AI, and offer practical ways for individuals to use technology responsibly to improve their communities and protect the environment.

Scenarios

  • Scenario 1: "As a software development student, how can I ensure the code I write in the future benefits society and doesn't harm vulnerable people?"
  • Scenario 2: "What are the environmental impacts of massive data centers, and how can I support 'green' technology initiatives?"
  • Scenario 3: "Where can I find resources to teach my children about critical thinking and media literacy so they aren't manipulated by algorithms?"

Color Schema

Primary Color: #1B4332 (Deep Forest Green)

Used for: Headings, Navigation background, and Footer background.

Secondary Color: #F4A261 (Warm Sand)

Used for: Accent lines, hover effects, and button backgrounds.

Background Color: #F8F9FA (Off-White)

Used for: The main body background to ensure readability.

Typography

  • Font 1: Montserrat (Bold) - Used for all headings (h1, h2, h3) to give the site a strong, modern, and trustworthy feel.
  • Font 2: Open Sans (Regular & Semi-Bold) - Used for all body text, paragraphs, and navigation links because it is highly readable on screens of all sizes.
