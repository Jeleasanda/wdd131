The Threat of Unchecked Technology

When technology is driven purely by profit and control, it can become a dangerous tool. Massive data centers consume vast amounts of energy, and algorithms are often designed to manipulate human behavior for financial gain.

Do you want to see the real danger?

The true danger is not that AI will hate us, but that humans will use AI to exploit other humans, destroying the Earth in the process for short-term profit.
